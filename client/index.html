A FIELD GUIDE TO GETTING LOST

One record can lead anywhere.

RabbitHole maps the artists, producers, and featured performers credited on records in MusicBrainz, the open music encyclopedia.

  1. 01

    Start with someone you love.

    Search for an artist, then choose the right result. Or open one of the featured maps.

  2. 02

    Follow a connection.

    Click any person or record to see their credits and a path back to your starting artist.

  3. 03

    Go down the rabbit hole.

    Choose “Dive deeper” to add more records and collaborators to your map. Drag nodes, zoom, and filter as you explore.

Credits vary between editions. We select notable records and show a limited set of production and featuring credits, not a complete discography. Choose “I like this” on a person to find new artists or producers. Artist recommendations use ListenBrainz listening patterns; producer recommendations use overlapping MusicBrainz production credits. Featured maps are cached snapshots; live dives read MusicBrainz directly. Cover art comes from the Cover Art Archive and biographies from Wikipedia.
